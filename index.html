Guided setup

Let the app walk you through a list of signs one by one. For each, make the correct ISL sign (check an ISL dictionary such as the ISLRTC one first), press Record, and hold the pose steadily. When you finish, press Export signs to download your own import file.

Teach a sign

Type the word this sign means, make the sign, then press Record. Record a few times with slightly different hand positions for better accuracy.
